## Full description (EN)

```
modplayjs plays tracker modules, Commodore 64 SID tunes and audio files that are already on your phone. Everything is local: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics. The app has no INTERNET permission at all.

WHAT IT PLAYS

MOD, S3M, XM, IT, MTM, STM, 669, MED/OctaMED, ICE, Asylum, DIGI Booster, Future Composer, MO3, Commodore 64 SID (PSID/RSID) and streamed WAV/MP3/OGG. Packed modules in the usual ProWizard formats are unpacked on load.

BUILT FOR LISTENING TO TRACKERS

- Library from the folders you grant, with search, format and channel filters, favourites and an ignore list
- Folder browser that opens at the file that is playing
- Realtime pattern view: every channel, the playing row followed, note / instrument / volume / effect columns in colours you choose
- Full-screen, monospace readers for the tracker message and the instrument and sample tables, so column layout survives
- Audio panel with volume, pan, interpolation, voice count, tempo and the mixer, reachable by swiping the player
- Channel mute and solo, instrument and sample audition
- SID options: chip model, filter, stereo, quality and CIA timing
- Repeat (off / all / one) and auto-advance
- Playback continues when the screen is off, with lock-screen and headset controls
- Playback resumes where it left off after the app was closed, even across a restart

CHOOSE HOW IT LOOKS

Light, dark or system theme, Material 3 Expressive, your own accent and pattern colours, English or German, adjustable text size.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

No online streaming, no account, no sync, no ads, no tracking. It reads the folders you pick and nothing else, and never writes to your files.

modplayjs is free software under the BSD-3-Clause license. The engine is a from-scratch port verified against the C reference players libxmp, OpenMPT and cRSID: byte-identical module data, 110/0 mixer-state goldens, bit-identical renders on the reference fixtures, and 0 load failures in every format those parsers also read.
```

## App content answers

- **Ads**: no. **In-app purchases**: no.
- **Data safety**: no data collected, no data shared. No `INTERNET`
  permission, no SDK that phones home.
- **Content rating**: utility/music player. No user-generated content, no
  user interaction, no ads, no location, no purchases.
- **Target audience**: 13+ / general. Not primarily for children.
- **Foreground service**: `mediaPlayback`, a permitted use.
- **Notifications**: `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` for the playback card.
- **Photo/video and files permission**: none; Storage Access Framework grants
  only.
