Privacy policy — modplayjs

modplayjs plays tracker modules, Commodore 64 SID tunes and audio files that are already on your device. It is a local player; there is no server, no account and no sync.

What the app accesses

  • Folders you pick. You add folders through Android's Storage Access Framework. The app stores the grant Android hands back, reads the file names and sizes to build its library, and reads a file only when you play it. It never writes to, deletes or modifies your files.
  • Playback state. Volume, queue and settings are stored on the device: a small Room database and a preferences file in the app's private storage. Android's backup can include them if you have device backup enabled.

What the app does not do

  • It has no INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere, and it contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or attribution SDK.
  • It does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, or usage data.
  • It does not record audio, take photos, or access the microphone or camera.
  • It does not share or sell anything, because it has nothing to share.

Permissions

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, WAKE_LOCK: keep playback running while the screen is off.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show the playback notification with its transport controls. Declining it only hides the notification.

Removing your data

Uninstall the app, or use Android's Clear storage for modplayjs. Both delete the library database and settings. Your music files are untouched.

Contact

Issues and questions: the repository at https://github.com/modplayjs/modplayjs.
